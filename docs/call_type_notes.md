# Notas de campo por tipo de llamada

Notas del protocolo de anotación: qué significa cada llamada y qué criterio usaron los
anotadores para dibujar la caja en Raven. No cubre todos los códigos de
`domain.species.CALL_TYPES`, solo los que tienen nota documentada.

## AA — night_monkey

- **gc** (gulp_call): Done to maintain the spatial cohesion of the group. Female.
  Selección: spaced calls. Low frequency. Consider the bottom point.
- **hm** (hoot_call): Done to maintain the spatial cohesion of the group. Male.
  Selección: spaced calls. Sounds like a heartbeat.
- **sc** (squeak_call): Warning call to acknowledge terrestrial danger.
  Selección: entire sequence. Looks like 2 or 3 vertical lines. Sounds like an insect.

## AC — peruvian_spider_monkey

- **chc** (chitter_call): Warning call to acknowledge terrestrial danger (researchers), not
  an alarm call. Selección: entire sequence, varying length.
- **gc** (growl_call): Interaction between two individuals associated with
  dominance/play. Mostly noticed in juvenile plays. Selección: entire sequence with
  harmonics, different length.
- **cc** (contact_call): Done to maintain cohesion in the group. Selección: spaced call,
  with harmonics.
- **whc** (whinnie_call): Long distance vocalization. Emitted in different circumstances:
  group movements, feeding, or contact maintenance. Selección: entire sequence with
  harmonics, different length.
- **bc** (bark_call): Passive: also a contact call. Aggressive: long and loud, a warning
  call, possible predator close by, or when juveniles lost the troop. Selección: spaced
  calls with harmonics.

## AS — howler_monkey

- **hc** (howl_call): Long territorial call. Selección: entire sequence.
- **bc** (bark_call): Warning call, sometimes happens in the introduction of the howl
  call. Selección: spaced calls.

## CC — shock_headed_capuchin_monkey

- **cc** (contact_call): Done to maintain cohesion in the group. Selección: spaced calls
  with harmonics.

## LW — weddells_saddleback_tamarin

- **cc** (contact_call): Done to maintain cohesion in the group; often followed by a
  response; 3-7 syllables. Selección: entire sequence, different lengths.
- **cs** (contact_syllable): Individual syllable of a contact_call (cc) sequence.
  Selección: individual syllable, nested inside its contact_call sequence.
- **aa** (aerial_alarm_call): Done to acknowledge aerial danger, no response. Selección:
  short sound; back tail longer than front.
- **ta** (terrestrial_alarm_call): Done to acknowledge terrestrial danger, no response.
  Selección: entire sequence; oscillating wave appearance.
- **sqc** (squeal_call): Squeals seem to reduce the hostility of conspecifics. Selección:
  isolated call, with harmonics.
- **phc** (phee_call): Vocalizations emitted during loose-contact interactions, likely
  from an immature individual. Selección: entire sequence.
- **tr** (trino_call): Contact calls over small distances. Selección: entire sequence,
  first syllable bigger than the rest, 2-4 syllables, variations.
- **tf** (trino_fast_call): Contact calls over small distances. Selección: entire
  sequence, last syllable length longer than the rest, variations.
- **tt** (trino_transition_call): Contact calls over small distances. Selección: entire
  sequence, first syllable length bigger than the rest, variations.
- **vc** (visual_contact_call): Visual acknowledgment, occurs during undisturbed social
  contexts. Selección: entire sequence.

## PT — toppins_titi_monkey

- **dc** (duet_call): Territorial call made in chorus by a male and female couple.
  Selección: entire sequence, mix of bellow phrases (straight lines - female) and pant
  phrases (tower - male). Select the harmonic too.
- **ac** (alarm_call): Functions as a deterrent signal to predators. Could also inform
  other individuals about location and distance of the predator. Selección: entire
  sequence, with harmonics.

## SB — bolivian_squirrel_monkey

- **pcc** (peep_contact_call): Done to maintain cohesion in the group. Selección: entire
  sequence. Can take different shapes. Different length.
- **ppc** (play_peep_call): Associated with movement and play. Selección: spaced calls.
  Can take different shapes.
- **lpc** (long_peep_call): Warning call, when they lose visual contact. Selección:
  isolated calls, entire sequence.
- **spc** (spit_call): The most used affiliative call. Selección: spaced calls, very high
  pitch sounds.
- **sc** (shriek_call): Extreme distress or fear. Individuals beaten in a fight or
  restrained. During or before an attack. Selección: isolated calls, very high pitch
  sounds.

## SM — large_headed_capuchin

- **cc** (contact_call): Done to maintain cohesion in the group. Selección: spaced calls
  with harmonics.
- **acc** (aggressive_contact_call): Produced during mild aggression directed at a close
  member. Could involve branch shaking, fixed stare, open mouth bared teeth. Alpha male
  and female adults towards juveniles and infants. Selección: entire sequence without
  harmonics.
- **sc** (squeal_call): Given in response to aggression. Selección: high variability in
  duration and frequency. Entire sequence, long series, select harmonics.
- **pc** (purr_call): Emitted when the troop is feeding on clumped resources. Selección:
  entire sequence.
- **whc** (whistle_call): Contact call. Selección: entire sequence. Different length.
- **hic** (hip_call): Warning call to acknowledge terrestrial danger. Selección: entire
  sequence. Spaced calls. Sound wave descending from left to right.
- **fc** (food_call): Foraging, food found in high concentration. Selección: entire
  sequence. Different lengths. Consider harmonics if clear enough. Careful with Trogon or
  Fasciated Antshrike sounds.
- **fs** (food_syllable): Individual syllable of a food_call (fc) sequence. Selección:
  individual syllable, nested inside its food_call sequence.
